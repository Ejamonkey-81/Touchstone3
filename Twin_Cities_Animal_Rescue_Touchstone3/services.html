Adoption and Programs

Whether you are ready to adopt, able to foster, or looking for a way to volunteer, there is more than one way to help an animal move toward a stable home.

Adoption Process

  1. Review available pets and submit an adoption inquiry.
  2. Talk with a rescue team member about the pet and your home.
  3. Meet the animal and ask questions before making a decision.
  4. Complete the adoption process and prepare for the pet to come home.

Foster Program

Foster homes give animals a quieter place to stay while they wait for adoption. The rescue provides guidance and stays in contact with foster families so they are not handling the process alone.

Volunteer Opportunities

Volunteers help with events, animal care support, transportation, outreach, and other needs that keep the rescue moving. Some roles are occasional while others work better for people who can help on a regular schedule.
